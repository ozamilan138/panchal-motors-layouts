Who we are

Panchal Motors

We build and customise vehicles for commercial and passenger use.

Our workshop takes each job from chassis and fabrication through to paint, interior and wiring. We build load bodies for pickups, ambulances, truck body work, bumpers and safety guards, and take on custom work.

We work on vehicles from Mahindra, Tata, Maruti, Hyundai, Toyota and Eicher.
