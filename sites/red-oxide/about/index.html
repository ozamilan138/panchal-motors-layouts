The workshop

Panchal Motors is a vehicle body-building workshop at Plot No. 1/B, GIDC, Railway Station Road, Deesa. We build and customise passenger and commercial vehicles, from chassis work to paint, interior and wiring.

Our work includes load, camper and steel bodies, ambulance builds, bumpers and safety guards, chassis fabrication, extension and repair, and paint and body work. Vehicle owners and fleet operators in and around Deesa bring their vehicles to us.

Why this red

Red oxide is the primer applied to bare steel before it is painted, to keep rust off. It is the colour of steel that has been built but not yet finished, which is why we chose it for this website.

How a build goes

The same six steps for every body we build, so you know where your vehicle is at each stage.

  1. Inspect the chassis

    We check the frame, axles and mounting points and tell you what it needs before any work starts.

  2. Agree the body

    Size, materials, doors and fittings are agreed with you before we cut any steel.

  3. Fabricate

    The frame and panels are cut, welded and fitted to your chassis in our workshop.

  4. Prime and paint

    Steel parts get a coat of primer, then the final colour goes on.

  5. Fit-out and wiring

    Interior, lights, wiring and accessories are fitted and checked.

  6. Handover

    We go over the finished vehicle with you before it leaves the workshop.
