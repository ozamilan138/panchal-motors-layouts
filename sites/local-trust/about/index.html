Who we are

Panchal Motors

We are a vehicle body-building workshop at GIDC, Deesa. We build and customise passenger and commercial vehicles, from chassis work to paint, interior and wiring.

Our work includes load bodies for pickups, ambulance builds, truck body work, bumpers, safety guards and custom fabrication.
