The workshop

Panchal Motors is a vehicle body-building workshop at GIDC, Deesa. We build and customise passenger and commercial vehicles: load bodies, ambulance builds, bumpers and guards, chassis work, paint, interior and wiring.

The chassis, body, paint and wiring are all done under one roof, so they are planned together from the first measurement.

We are open Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm. Bring the vehicle in, or send photos on WhatsApp first.

How a build goes

Every body follows the same six steps, so you know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Measure the vehicle

    We measure your chassis and talk through the load.

    Bring the vehicle to the workshop, or send photos and the model on WhatsApp first.

  2. 2

    Agree the drawing

    You approve the size and layout before any steel is cut.

    This is the time to change the length, height or layout.

  3. 3

    Fabricate the body

    Frame, panels and any chassis work, built in our workshop.

    If the body needs a longer or stronger frame, the chassis work is done first.

  4. 4

    Paint and finish

    Surface preparation and paint, in the colour you choose.

    Body work and paint are done in the same workshop.

  5. 5

    Wiring and fit-out

    Lights, wiring, interior and accessories fitted.

    Ambulance interiors and lighting are fitted at this stage.

  6. 6

    Handover

    We go over the finished vehicle with you at the workshop.

    Ask anything you need to before you drive away.

Why we measure first

Two vehicles of the same model can carry very different loads on very different roads. So every body starts with your vehicle and your job, not a standard size.

  • Body length is set by the load you carry and the chassis behind the cab.
  • Height is set by your load and the routes you drive.
  • Rear overhang: if the body needs to be longer than the chassis allows, the chassis is extended first.
  • Bumpers and guards are made to fit your vehicle's own mounting points.
