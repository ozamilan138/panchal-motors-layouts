The process

Seven stages, one workshop.

  1. 01Consultation
  2. 02Design
  3. 03Material preparation
  4. 04Fabrication
  5. 05Painting
  6. 06Finishing
  7. 07Delivery
01

Consultation

We start with your vehicle, what it has to carry, how it will be used and what you want fitted.

02

Design

The body layout, dimensions and fittings are planned around the chassis.

03

Material preparation

Steel sections and sheet are cut and prepared for the body.

04

Fabrication

The frame, body, guards or bumper are fabricated, with chassis work where the build needs it.

05

Painting

Body work and paint finish the exterior of the build.

06

Finishing

Interior work, wiring and accessories are fitted to complete the vehicle.

07

Delivery

The finished vehicle is handed back, ready for work.

What we build

Pickup and load bodies, ambulances, truck bodies, bumpers and safety guards — for Mahindra, Tata, Maruti, Hyundai, Toyota and Eicher vehicles.
